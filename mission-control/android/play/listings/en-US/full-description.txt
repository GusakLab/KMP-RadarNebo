MissionControl-Nebo is the operator's companion for a RadarNebo server. It reads the server's public health report and shows, at a glance, whether the server is running and getting fresh data.

• Status: running and getting data, waiting for the first data, stale data, or a failing data source
• Data source: alerts.in.ua or the simulation, and the current data version
• Situation: active alerts, active and total tracks, and how old the data is
• Upstream: requests in the last minute against the budget, totals, rate-limited, rejected and failed requests
• Polling: interval, last and next poll, and the last error
• Refreshes every 5 seconds while the app is open; pull a fresh report any time
• Watch any RadarNebo server: the public one or your own, on the internet or on your local network
• English, Ukrainian and Dutch

The app reads only the server's /health report. It needs no account, collects no personal data and contains no ads.
